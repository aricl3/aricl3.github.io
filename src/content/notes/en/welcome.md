I wanted a durable digital space that I could shape and own over time.

Social platforms are useful for quick conversations, but knowledge and projects deserve lasting context. This site will collect three kinds of work:

## Technical notes

I'll document problems, experiments, and lessons from building AI agents, RAG systems, LLM evaluations, and production software. The goal is to explain not only *how*, but *why*.

## Project retrospectives

The valuable part of a project is rarely the final screenshot. It is how the problem was framed, how the design evolved, and which assumptions were overturned by evidence.

## Evolving ideas

I'll also keep shorter notes on learning, collaboration, and product building—hopefully forming a useful trail of thought over the years.
